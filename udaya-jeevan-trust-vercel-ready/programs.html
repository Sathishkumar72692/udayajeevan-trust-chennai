01 / Women empowerment

Confidence, collective strength and choice

We support women’s groups and learning opportunities that can help participants make informed decisions, strengthen networks and explore sustainable income.

SHG formation & supportGroup participation, leadership and peer learning.
Skills & financial literacyTraining in practical skills, budgeting and savings.
Enterprise pathwaysInformation on suitable schemes, including KULU Fund and Mudra loans, subject to eligibility and partner availability.
Rights & awarenessSessions on inclusion, safety and access to services.

Intended outcomes: stronger confidence, improved access to information and more livelihood choices.

03 / Health & welfare

Health information, care and connection

We aim to improve awareness and connect community members with appropriate health information and services.

Community health campsHealth screening and referral partnerships where available.
Maternal healthAwareness about antenatal and postnatal wellbeing.
Menstrual hygieneDignified, practical education and access awareness.

We support learning and connections that help people explore small enterprises and income-generating activities.

Micro-enterprise readinessBusiness basics, costing, customer care and planning.
Digital & e-commerce skillsProduct presentation, digital payments and online selling basics.
Market connectionsOpportunities to connect with buyers and local networks.
Institutional partnershipsExploring appropriate collaboration with NABARD, SIDBI and other partners; no affiliation is implied.
