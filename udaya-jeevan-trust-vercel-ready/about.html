Our story

People and potential at the heart of our work

Udaya Jeevan Trust is a public charitable trust established in Perambur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Our work focuses on women’s empowerment, child development, health and welfare, livelihood promotion and community awareness.

We believe that lasting progress grows through trust, participation and practical support. We aim to work with communities—not simply for them—so that programmes reflect real needs, local knowledge and shared aspirations.

As the trust grows, we seek to strengthen collaboration with educational institutions, public bodies, responsible businesses and community organisations.

What guides us

Vision, mission and values

◎

Our vision

Communities where women can participate fully, children can learn and flourish, and every family can pursue a healthy, secure and sustainable future.

↗

Our mission

To strengthen community capacity through education, health awareness, skills development, livelihood support and partnerships that create meaningful opportunities.

✳

Our values

Dignity, inclusion, transparency, compassion, accountability and respect for the knowledge and agency of every community.
